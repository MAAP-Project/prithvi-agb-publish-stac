About this map

These layers come from NASA-IBM Prithvi-EO-2.0, a geospatial foundation model fine-tuned on ICESat-2 ATL08 canopy and biomass retrievals for 2020. Inputs are Harmonized Landsat Sentinel-2 summer reflectance and Copernicus GLO-30 terrain; the model predicts biomass density, vegetation height and their uncertainties at 30 m.

Predictions cover 4,940 tiles (about 31 million km²) on the same 90 km grid as the ICESat-2 boreal v3.1 product, so the two maps compare pixel for pixel. Non-vegetated surfaces (water, built, bare, ice) are masked with ESA WorldCover.

Tiles are served live by TiTiler from cloud-optimized GeoTIFFs catalogued in the MAAP STAC (browse the collections). Prepared at the University of Alabama in Huntsville with NASA MSFC.

Layers render from zoom 6 in; zoom into the boreal to see data. Click the map for biomass and height values at that pixel. Gaps inside the domain are masked water, built, bare and ice.
